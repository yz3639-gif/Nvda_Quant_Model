# Model card — NVDA Research Platform v2

**Reference experiment:** 2026-09-18. **Documentation review:** 2026-09-23. **Primary market-data cutoff:** 2026-05-22. These dates describe different events; this publication is not a refreshed-market or live-performance result.

## Intended use

Daily NVDA equity forecasting and strategy research, reproducibility, and demonstration of research/engineering decisions. The unit of study is a daily directional signal and its trading policy, with a separate predictive-distribution comparison. The registered experiment does not price NVDA options, calibrate an implied-volatility surface or demonstrate commodity-option market making. Outputs are not an autonomous order system or proof of profitable live execution.

## Models and policies

The controlled experiment compares a fixed volume/momentum rule, logistic regression (two registered regularization settings), and shallow histogram boosting (two registered depths). Tuning uses a chronological inner window. Probability mappings are raw, shrinkage to a training base rate, and sigmoid; a mapping is selected inside the training period and assessed on later outer folds. Refit transfer can change calibration, which is why the outer scores matter.

The full [registered configuration](configs/full.json) contains **11 candidates: five settings across these three directional families plus six distribution variants**. The frozen market run has **18 outer folds** and **1,075 predictions per family**. Each outer fold targets 63 sessions after a 504-session training window; the last fold may be shorter. Inputs contain 12 return, volatility, range, volume and moving-average features. This finite design is a control on the current experiment, not proof that the broader historical search was limited to 11 candidates.

The probability benchmark is the training-period event frequency with smoothing. All probabilities and active-subset scores are retained, not only the selected strategy's winning trades. A 0.55 threshold is fixed before the run. Fixed, volatility-targeted and training-estimated conditional-size controls distinguish probability changes from lower exposure. Exposure controls are estimates from prior validation and do not claim exact future volatility/beta matching.

Distribution models are moving-block bootstrap, Normal log-return dynamics, and standardized Student-t log shocks, each with rolling or EWMA volatility. The Normal SDE drift correctly converts estimated log drift before applying the simulator's Ito correction. Evaluation covers 21 and 5 sessions, interval score, coverage, interval width, both tails and PIT. Primary horizons do not overlap; overlapping 21-session forecasts are supplemental and carry HAC uncertainty. Shared fitting histories and historical model selection still limit inference. Student-t log shocks do not justify a finite expected price; no price-mean or option-pricing claim is made from that model.

The historical strict/default strategies are a separate frozen-signal execution replay. Their original feature construction and model selection are not regenerated by that replay.

## Execution, costs and accounting

A close(t) decision predicts the direction of the return from open(t+1) to open(t+2). Training rows are eligible only after their complete labels have matured. Actual strategy holding periods may differ because of stop/take exits, position changes and drawdown halts; probability accuracy is therefore not the same quantity as trading win rate or net P&L.

The primary backtest uses next-session-open execution, entry-referenced stops, observed-open fills for gaps and a stop-first convention when both intrabar barriers are touched. Daily OHLC cannot establish the true intrabar order or an executable bid/ask. Baseline costs are **10 basis points commission plus 5 basis points slippage per side**, charged on absolute traded notional. Tests at **1×, 2× and 4×** are simulation assumptions, not measured venue costs or an order-book capacity model.

The backtest reconciles `equity = cash + shares × mark = initial capital + realized P&L + unrealized P&L`. Fees are retained per fill and included in trading episodes. The 17 saved strategy/control variants, 51 cost cases and 36 stop/take neighbors preserve losing outcomes and halted periods. Exposure controls use training estimates; positive results from a low-turnover control are not evidence of directional model skill.

## Data and evaluation

The reference market experiment uses **1,641 daily rows from 2019-11-11 through 2026-05-22**. Its outer trading evaluation spans **2022-02-07 through 2026-05-21**. The separately replayed default result ends **2026-07-24**. Historical adjusted-price vintages and vendor release times are not independently certified. These dates are not the current market date. A fresh download creates a different input vintage and requires a new run identity.

Extensive prior candidate searches contaminated the historical selection process. New chronological folds provide development diagnostics, not an untouched final holdout. Block bootstrap/HAC summaries are descriptive and not adjusted for the old selection process. No candidate is automatically promoted because it leads a table. Prospective shadow records require fresh data and an immutable creation clock; no prospective track record is asserted here.

The event demonstration contains only 26 records over roughly three months, with insufficient historical availability evidence. It fails the minimum 250-event/24-month/source/availability gates. Those gates would still not guarantee adequate statistical power. A news-alpha ablation is therefore not promoted as completed empirical validation.

## Failure cases and limitations

- Execution timing and stop reference can materially change historical results; next-open/entry is a strategy assumption change as well as an execution change.
- Daily OHLC does not reveal the high/low ordering, queue position or achievable intraday liquidity.
- Permanent drawdown halts can create long flat sections; those must not be interpreted as sustained active low-risk performance.
- A small Brier improvement can be noise and need not produce profitable trading after costs.
- A lower drawdown caused by reduced exposure is not an independent alpha signal.
- Interval coverage alone can be improved by widening intervals; interval scores and widths must also be considered.
- Stale/missing news is context only; missing historical releases cannot be made point-in-time by assigning today's ingestion time.
- The project includes older exploratory modules. The registered v2 runner is the reproducible research path; it does not retroactively validate every exploratory optimizer or every live-data adapter.
